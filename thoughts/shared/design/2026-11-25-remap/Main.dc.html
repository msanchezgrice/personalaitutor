How the map gets drawn

Three steps, in this order. The first two are free.

1. Tell us what you did

Paste your résumé or describe your last job. We read what you actually did, line by line, and ignore the title.

2. We translate it

Each thing you did is matched to the AI-era skill it has become, with the line from your résumé that backs it up.

3. You get a new title and a route

One title that fits best, two alternates, the gaps to close, and the three pieces of proof that will make it believable.

Questions people ask first

Will this get me a job?

Nobody can promise that. What you get is a title that fits what you have actually done, and proof a hiring manager can open and check.

How do you choose the new title?

From the work in your résumé, not from your old title. Every translated skill shows the line it came from, so you can see the reasoning and disagree with it.

Is the model really tuned on my work?

Yes, in context: we build a voice profile from your samples and run a base model with your examples. Your model card states the method, the base model and the number of samples.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The routes start from marketing, design, production, QA, support and recruiting work. Your tutor walks you through each build.

What happens to my résumé?

We use it to draw your map and nothing else. Your map is private until you choose to share the link.
