# Career Remapping Workbook

## From past work to your next proof project

My AI Skill Tutor · Version 1 · October 2026

You already have a work history. Use this workbook to investigate which parts may transfer, compare two directions, and define a small project that makes your next move more credible.

**The method:** actual tasks → transferable capabilities → two possible directions → one important gap → one proof project → a truthful career story.

Take a first pass in 20–30 minutes, then return with current role descriptions and project evidence. This is a suggested starting session, not a promise of a completed career plan. No resume upload is needed. Keep private details in your own copy.

## 6. Write a career story you can defend

“My background is in ____________________. I have experience doing ____________________. I am exploring ____________________ because it uses ____________________. My recent independent project demonstrates ____________________ under these conditions: ____________________. I am still learning ____________________.”

For a project resume bullet, use **built/did + specific task + output/check + scope**. Add measured outcomes only when you have the baseline and result.

**Illustrative wording, usable only after comparable work is complete:** “Built a local checker for synthetic support replies, with required-field and escalation checks, reproducible cases, and a documented failure report.”

Do not rename a past job, invent AI employment, or imply production deployment from a local demonstration. Keep independent projects separate from employment history.
