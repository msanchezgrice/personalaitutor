## Hero

### Your experience still gives you somewhere to start.

When your work changes, it can be hard to see where your skills fit next. Use the Career Remapping Workbook to turn what you have done into a small, testable plan for what comes next.

**Primary button: Get the workbook**

Start with five tasks, compare two directions, and choose one proof project. Use your own copy; no resume upload is needed.

## What you will work through

- An inventory of actual tasks and the capabilities they may demonstrate.
- A comparison of two directions using current role requirements and your constraints.
- A project brief with completion checks, evidence, and limitations.
- A truthful resume or interview story connecting past work to recent practice.

## Built for people whose work is changing

You may be adapting within a role, returning to the job market, or exploring work outside games, design, marketing, product, analytics, or operations. Start with your tasks. Correct the connections and choose a different direction whenever it fits better.

## See the method in one example

**Fictional example:** A games QA professional maps defect reproduction to case design, investigates software quality work, and builds a checker using synthetic support replies. The project supports a specific claim about that checker and the person's decisions. It does not automatically qualify them for AI engineering.
